# Episode Idea Bank: Season 1 (Raghu, age 10)

Each idea: **hardship → deity (form) → what Raghu must do himself → moral → deity sign**.

| # | Title | Hardship | Deity (form) | Raghu's own choice | Moral | Sign left behind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The First Obstacle** | Raghu's only school shoes tear on exam day and he's afraid to go | Ganesha (plump sweet-shop owner) | Goes to school barefoot and writes his exam with confidence | Obstacles test you; they don't stop you | A modak in his bag |
| 2 | **The Bully at the Gate** | Vikram takes Raghu's lunch every day | Hanuman (monkey on the school wall) | Stands tall and says "no", then shares his lunch with Vikram later | Courage is standing up, not hitting back | A saffron thread on the gate |
| 3 | **The Found Wallet** | Appa can't pay the rent; Raghu finds a wallet full of money | Lakshmi (lady at Amma's stall) | Returns the wallet to its owner | Honest wealth stays, stolen wealth leaves | Lotus petals on the doorstep |
| 4 | **The Lie** | Raghu breaks the neighbour's window and Kiran gets blamed | Krishna (flute-playing cowherd boy) | Confesses the truth | Truth feels heavy for a moment, lies for a lifetime | A peacock feather |
| 5 | **Silent Voice** | Raghu freezes on stage at the school speech competition | Saraswati (old music teacher) | Speaks from his heart instead of memorising | Your true voice is enough | A white swan feather |
| 6 | **Appa's Fever** | Appa is sick and can't drive; no income for the week | Hanuman (strong market porter) | Helps Amma deliver her tailoring orders across town | Strength is serving the family | Sound of temple bells |
| 7 | **Tommy is Gone** | His street dog Tommy dies | Shiva (calm sadhu by the river) | Lets go and gives Tommy's food bowl to a new puppy | Love doesn't end; it changes form | Faint damaru sound |
| 8 | **The Unfair Punishment** | Meena Teacher punishes Raghu for something he didn't do | Durga (lady police officer at school) | Calmly and respectfully tells the truth to the headmaster | Stand for justice, even when you are small | A red hibiscus flower |
| 9 | **Jealous Eyes** | Kiran gets a new bicycle; Raghu feels jealous and says mean things | Krishna (cowherd boy) | Apologises and celebrates his friend | Joy shared is joy doubled | A peacock feather |
| 10 | **Exam Fail** | Raghu fails maths and hides the report card | Ganesha (mouse Mooshika leads him) | Shows Appa and asks for help | Failure is the first step, not the last | A modak |
| 11 | **Monsoon Flood** | Rain floods the house; their things are ruined | Shiva / Ganga (old man at the ghat) | Helps neighbours before himself | In storms, be someone's shelter | Crescent-moon reflection in the water |
| 12 | **Vikram's Secret** | Raghu discovers the bully is beaten at home | Durga + Hanuman | Befriends Vikram and tells Paati, who helps | Hurt people hurt people; kindness heals | Lion paw print in mud |
