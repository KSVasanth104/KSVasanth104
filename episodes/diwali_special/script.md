# Diwali Special: "Happy Diwali from the Gods"

**Format:** YouTube Shorts / Reels, vertical 9:16, 60 seconds, 11 shots.
**Idea:** On Diwali night, Raghu lights the first diya at the family pooja shelf and whispers,
"Will you come for Diwali?" One by one, each deity appears in their **true divine form**, in their
own world, and wishes the viewer "Happy Diwali" in their own style. The finale returns to the
reason we celebrate: Rama and Sita coming home to Ayodhya.

**Reverence notes (section 4 of the bible):** every deity is in true form, with accurate
iconography, a soft glow and dignified framing (low or eye-level camera, never comic). No
deity is ranked above another. Each gets the same screen time and the same respectful,
symmetrical framing. Ganesha opens because every auspicious beginning starts with him.

> Rama and Sita are **new to the deity cast**. Their descriptions follow the most widely
> recognised depiction. Add them to the bible's `--oref` table once you have their images.

## Edit notes (CapCut)

- **Transitions:** a diya-flame flash (white-gold glow) between every deity shot, synced to a
  single temple-bell hit.
- **Music:** soft shehnai or flute bed for shots 01–09, rising to full festive dhol and bells for
  shots 10–11.
- **Sound effects:** match strike (01), Mooshika squeak (02), coins chiming (03), veena (04),
  flute and sparkler fizz (05), wind and flag (06), lion's low growl (07), one damaru beat (08),
  peacock call (09), crowd cheering and bells (10), firecrackers in the distance (11).
- **On-screen text:** each deity's name in a small gold title at the bottom for the first second
  of their shot. End card: "Happy Diwali from Raghu and family".
