# God Is Near (working title)

An AI cartoon series about Raghu, an ordinary Indian boy, and the Hindu deities who physically step
into his life during his hardest moments, guiding him but never making his choices for him.

- **Show bible:** [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md)
- **Episode ideas:** [episodes/ideas.md](episodes/ideas.md)

Tools: Midjourney (images and video), ElevenLabs (voices), CapCut (final edit), Claude Code (writing, prompts, automation).
